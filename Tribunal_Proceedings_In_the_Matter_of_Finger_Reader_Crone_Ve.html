Tribunal Proceedings: In the Matter of Finger Reader Crone Vestia

by March

TRIBUNAL PROCEEDINGS: IN THE MATTER OF FINGER READER CRONE VESTIA

Ecclesiastical Court, Roundtable Hold Annex

Presiding: Deacon-Arbiter Holst, Office of the Two Fingers.

Prosecution: Advocate Lendrel, Office of Golden Fundamentalism.

Defense: None appointed. The accused represents herself.

Scribe: Clerk Aldric. These proceedings are recorded verbatim.

CHARGE

Finger Reader Crone Vestia is charged with Fabrication of Divine Interpretation, a Category 2 offense under the Doctrine of Faithful Relay (Year 327). Specifically: that between the Month of the Stag, Year 409, and the Month of the Boar, Year 411, the accused provided no fewer than forty-three interpretations of Two Fingers communications that the Two Fingers did not produce.

BACKGROUND

The Two Fingers communicate through movement. The movements are involuntary, arhythmic, and slow. A single communicative gesture may take hours to complete. The movements are interpreted by a caste of trained readers — the Finger Reader Crones — who translate the movements into language that can be conveyed to the faithful.

The interpretation process is not standardized. There is no codebook. There is no grammar. Each Finger Reader develops her own method through decades of observation. The Office of the Two Fingers has never published a guide to interpretation because the Office's position is that interpretation is a divinely granted skill that cannot be reduced to method. The Office has also never audited an interpretation, for the same reason.

This tribunal is the first audit.

PROSECUTION'S CASE

Advocate Lendrel presents the following evidence.

Between Year 409 and Year 411, the Two Fingers stationed at the Roundtable Hold produced observable movement on fourteen occasions. These fourteen movements were documented by a mechanical recording device installed by the Office of Golden Fundamentalism as part of an unrelated research project on divine communication frequency.

The mechanical device is a weight-and-pulley system attached to the Two Fingers' resting surface. It records gross motion — movement exceeding one-quarter inch in any direction — as a mark on a wax cylinder. The cylinder rotates once per day. Marks on the cylinder correspond to days on which the Two Fingers moved.

Fourteen days show marks. Fourteen days, the Two Fingers moved.

Finger Reader Crone Vestia provided interpretations on forty-three days during the same period.

Twenty-nine of those interpretations were delivered on days when the Two Fingers did not move.

VESTIA'S RESPONSE

Vestia states that the Two Fingers communicate through more than gross motion. She states that the mechanical device records movement but not tremor, not warmth variation, not the quality she describes as "pressure" — a change in the air near the Fingers that has no physical correlate she can name but which she has felt for the thirty-seven years of her practice.

She states that on many of the twenty-nine disputed days, she felt the pressure change. She interpreted the change. She provided the interpretation to whoever was waiting.

Advocate Lendrel asks whether "pressure" is documented in any training material.

Vestia states that there is no training material. She learned from Reader Crone Moira, who learned from Reader Crone Elspeth, who learned from a reader whose name is not recorded. The skill is transmitted by proximity. The student sits with the Fingers for years before she begins to feel anything. What she feels first is the gross motion. What she feels later is finer. What she feels after twenty years is what Vestia calls "the hum."

The hum has no sound. It is not auditory. It is, Vestia says, the feeling of being in a room where someone is about to speak. She has felt it since Year 393. It does not always precede movement. Sometimes it is all there is.

Advocate Lendrel asks whether it is possible that the hum is not a communication from the Two Fingers but a sensation produced by Vestia's own expectation.

Vestia says she has considered this.

Advocate Lendrel asks what her conclusion was.

Vestia says she does not have one. She says that after thirty-seven years, she cannot distinguish between the Two Fingers preparing to communicate and her own readiness to receive a communication. She says that the distinction may not exist. She says that this is either a failure of her practice or the deepest truth of it, and she has not determined which.

TESTIMONY: CONTENT OF THE DISPUTED INTERPRETATIONS

Advocate Lendrel presents a log of the forty-three interpretations provided during the disputed period. The log was maintained by the Hold's reception clerk, who records each interpretation as delivered.

Of the fourteen interpretations that coincide with verified Two Fingers movement, the content is consistent with historical patterns: guidance for specific Tarnished ("proceed to the Altus Plateau," "seek the Academy"), general theological advisories ("the Erdtree's grace flows eastward this season"), and one warning ("do not enter the capital at this time").

Of the twenty-nine interpretations that do not coincide with verified movement, the content is also consistent with historical patterns. There is no detectable difference in subject, tone, or specificity between the verified and unverified interpretations. Advocate Lendrel states that this consistency is evidence of skilled fabrication: a competent forger would match the style of genuine communications.

Vestia states that the consistency is evidence that all forty-three interpretations came from the same source.

Advocate Lendrel asks her to clarify.

Vestia says: "The Fingers are dying."

The Deacon-Arbiter requests elaboration.

Vestia says that when she began her practice, a communicative gesture took between two and four hours. By Year 400, the average had increased to eight hours. By Year 405, twelve hours. The most recent verified movement — the warning about the capital — took thirty-one hours to complete. The intervals between movements have lengthened from weeks to months.

She says the Fingers are slowing. She says that what the mechanical device measures is the last stage of a process that begins much earlier. The hum, the pressure, the warmth variation — these are the early stages. They are the Fingers trying to move. Sometimes they succeed. Sometimes the message begins but the body cannot complete it. On those days, Vestia interprets what she can feel of the attempt.

She says she does not know how much longer the Fingers will move at all.

Advocate Lendrel asks whether this claim can be verified.

Vestia says the mechanical device has been in place for two years. She says that if the Office installs a more sensitive device — one that measures tremor and thermal variation — it will record what she has been feeling for three decades.

The Office of Golden Fundamentalism has not authorized such a device. The Deacon-Arbiter asks why. Advocate Lendrel states that the research project concerns communication frequency, not communication mechanism. Expanding the project's scope would require a new authorization from the Synod. The Synod's schedule is full. Estimated wait time for a new research authorization: eighteen months.

Vestia is seventy-one years old. She is the only Finger Reader currently assigned to the Roundtable Hold. Her apprentice, Reader-Novice Dara, has completed four years of proximity training. Reader-Novice Dara reports that she can feel the gross motion but not yet the tremor and not the hum. She estimates another ten to fifteen years before she is ready to interpret independently. Vestia has not disputed this estimate.

DEACON-ARBITER'S FINDING

The charge is Fabrication of Divine Interpretation. The standard of proof is: did the accused knowingly present invented content as a communication from the Two Fingers?

The evidence establishes that twenty-nine interpretations were delivered on days when no verified movement occurred. The evidence does not establish that the Two Fingers were not communicating on those days. It establishes that the device did not detect movement. These are different facts.

The accused has practiced for thirty-seven years without prior complaint. The content of the disputed interpretations is indistinguishable from verified communications. The accused has offered a plausible, though unverifiable, account of a communication mechanism that the Office's equipment was not designed to detect.

The Deacon-Arbiter finds insufficient evidence to sustain the charge.

The Deacon-Arbiter notes that this finding does not validate the accused's account of sub-threshold communication. It does not authorize future interpretations based on tremor, warmth, or "pressure." It does not recommend the installation of additional measuring equipment. It finds only that the Office has not proven fabrication.

The accused is released to her duties. The mechanical device will remain in place. The log of interpretations will continue. The Office may re-examine the matter at any time if further evidence is obtained.

Reader-Novice Dara has been directed to maintain her own log of what she observes during the Fingers' quiescent periods. This directive was not requested by either party. The Deacon-Arbiter issues it on his own authority. The log will be sealed and stored with the tribunal record. It will not be reviewed unless a future tribunal requires it.

The Deacon-Arbiter does not state his reasons for this directive. The reasons are not part of the finding.
