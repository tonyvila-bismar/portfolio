Current volume: Year of the Erdtree 408 through present.

Registrar: Deacon Aldous, Castle Morne Chapel.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This registry tracks Misbegotten births, physical assessments, and labor assignments within the Castle Morne jurisdiction. Misbegotten are classified under the Golden Order's Edict on Impure Lineages (Year 304) as "beasts of labor bearing Crucible-residual morphology." They are registered under the Office of Pastoral Husbandry, which also maintains records for cattle, draft horses, and war dogs.

The registry uses the Office's standard livestock format. No alternative format has been developed for Misbegotten because no alternative classification has been proposed. A request was submitted to the Office of Golden Fundamentalism in Year 371 by Deacon Aldous's predecessor, Deacon Maren, asking whether a separate registry category might be appropriate given that Misbegotten possess language, tool use, and what Deacon Maren described as "evident emotional capacity." The request was returned with a notation that the classification had been settled by the Edict and that the Edict did not require revision.

Deacon Maren retired the following year.

Entry 3.

Foaling date: 22nd of the Month of the Stag.

Dam: Misbegotten female, registry number CM-0619. Assigned to masonry support (hauling). Age: approximately 27 years.

Sire: Unknown.

Offspring: Two. Male and female. Both viable. Combined weight: approximately 11 pounds. Twin births occur in approximately 1 in 40 Misbegotten deliveries, which is three times the rate observed in the general human population. The Office of Pastoral Husbandry attributes the higher rate to Crucible-residual biology. The attribution is speculative. No study has been conducted.

Morphological assessment (male): standard Misbegotten phenotype, no wings, pronounced scaling across shoulders and upper back. Scaling is cosmetic and does not impede function.

Morphological assessment (female): standard Misbegotten phenotype, no wings, no visible Crucible traits beyond baseline facial structure.

Labor assignment: Both pending maturation.

Notes: The female offspring's lack of visible Crucible traits beyond baseline has been noted. In rare cases — approximately 1 in 200 births in this registry — a Misbegotten offspring presents with minimal morphological deviation from the human norm. The Office's policy on such cases is that classification follows parentage, not phenotype. A Misbegotten's offspring is Misbegotten regardless of appearance. The offspring will be registered, housed, and assigned accordingly.

The previous registrar, Deacon Maren, maintained a separate informal log of minimal-deviation births. The log recorded 14 such cases over a twenty-year period. In three of those cases, the offspring were removed from the Misbegotten nursery by Castle Morne household staff and placed with human families in the Weeping Peninsula villages. Deacon Maren's log describes these removals as "reassignments." The Office of Pastoral Husbandry's records describe them as "inventory discrepancies." No investigation was conducted. The three children are not tracked in any registry maintained by this office.

Deacon Maren's informal log was found in her personal effects after her retirement. It is not an official document. It is stored in this office's miscellaneous file.

REGISTRAR'S ANNUAL ADDENDUM

The registry form does not include a section for general observations. This addendum is supplementary and is maintained at the registrar's discretion.

Castle Morne's Misbegotten population has been stable for approximately forty years. Birth rates slightly exceed death rates. The excess is sold or transferred to other jurisdictions approximately every five years, when the population exceeds the labor capacity of the castle's operations. The last transfer occurred in Year 407, when 30 Misbegotten were sold to the Altus Plateau mining consortium at a rate of 8 runes per head.

The Misbegotten at Castle Morne do not have names in this registry. They have numbers. Some of them have names among themselves. The nursery handler, who has worked in the Misbegotten nursery for eleven years, knows most of these names. She is not Misbegotten. She is a human woman from the peninsula village of Southmere. She was hired because the previous nursery handler quit, and she was the only applicant. She calls the infants by their names when she feeds them and by their numbers when she files the health reports.

I have not included the names in this registry because the form does not have a field for them and because adding a field would require authorization from the Office of Pastoral Husbandry. I submitted this request in Year 409. It has not been acknowledged.

CM-0774's son, the one with the green eyes, is called Wren by his dam. I know this because I was present at the birth, as the form requires, and the dam said the name aloud before the nursery handler arrived, and I heard it, and the form does not have a field for what the registrar heard.
